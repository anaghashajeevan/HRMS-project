6. Is the subnet overlap the cause?
Yes. The overlap is the direct root cause, and it is broader than the backend container. Because the 192.168.0.0/20 route lives in the shared Docker host namespace, it black-holes access to the entire company LAN for all containers, not just the three on that network.
- Backend on the conflicting bridge gets EHOSTUNREACH because 192.168.1.19 is treated as on-link.
- Containers on agentos-net, nlts-pm, digi-kaizen, and marketing time out because their packets are routed toward the conflicting bridge instead of eth0.
- Internet access still works, confirming the default Docker route is healthy.
Fixing the subnet should restore LAN access for the backend and the connector. Confidence is high because the host/WSL path to the device already works.
